## Brake / duct scope note

Tire-side brake coupling only: native BeamNG owns rotor thermals and fade. Node-Thermal Friction soaks rim/carcass from stock brake temps; duct sliders cool tire/rim air and modulate soak — they do not write native rotor cooling coefficients.
