# Verification

Index of which file owns each claim: `DOCUMENTATION.md`. After a drive, add a row to `history/CHANGELOG.md`. A lock also goes in `history/LOCKS.md`. A rejected change goes in `history/FAILURES.md`. After that review is written, run `tools/scripts/Copy-NtfLogs.ps1` so the dated copy on `D:\ntf-logs` matches the run that was read.

Read this before a drive, a retune, or calling a pass done. Maturity belongs to one claim. A whole phase can be smoked while one flag inside it is still only coded.

## Ladder

1. **Coded.** It loads. It decides nothing.
2. **Soft-sim.** A script PASS. The gate scripts follow the live per-tire gates. `Test-ReplayRecordedThermal.ps1` runs the real thermal module outside the game. Its stub car, and the golden check's, must be seated (`playerInfo.firstPlayerSeated`), or the module stops at the lite step. A script PASS is still not a drive verdict.
3. **Smoke.** One live drive, judged by feel. A smoke can reject a change. It cannot lock a number. The GT3 is smoke-only for heat.
4. **Logged.** A CSV answers the question that was asked. One route cannot retune a locked band.
5. **A/B.** One switch, same car, same maneuver, against a pass you already accepted.
6. **Locked.** The number stays until that same protocol misses.

## What each step may change

- Smoke: stop, or keep going. No number edits.
- Log: answer the question on the card. No locked-number edits.
- A/B of an enable flag: keep the flag, or leave it off. The locked scales stay.
- Lock: only after the protocol that owns that number. After that lock, commit it and push it to the private repo `Node-Thermal-Friction-private` on `testing/main` and `main`. Do not wait for the public repo. An edit that is not locked stays local.
- Axle edit: the standard. A miss on one axle changes that axle's scale. A global step waits until both axles share the miss.

## Numbers

The logger sets the bound. A scale moves from that log. Feeling is the fine adjustment inside the bound, and it is written beside the number. A smoke can still reject a change. It cannot set the bound. When a logged settled median misses the open card's target by more than that track's floor, edit that card's knob in the same review. The step is the smallest one whose predicted shift clears the floor and moves toward the target. Locked scales stay. Do not wait to be asked.

Slick and sport aim at realistic grip windows and temperatures. Wide plateaus from the feeling phase stay retired. A log that sits cold of a realistic window is a heat result. The plateau is not widened to cover it.

## Heat balance

Shape one generator at a time, inside the realistic window. Each step is its own logged run. It takes many runs.

1. **Straightaway median.** If cruise is cold of the window, raise straight-line heat (load·ω rolling). If cruise is hot of the window, lower that term. Corner heat and cooling stay. If the undriven tires finish outside the window, rolling heat goes up. Driven tires do not set that increase.
2. **Corner balance.** If the corner peak sits over the hot side and stays there, lower corner heat (slip and work). A peak that falls back inside the plateau within a few seconds is an acceptable flash. `over` is the seconds above the hot edge. `hold` is the seconds within 2°C of that peak. Straight-line heat stays at the step-1 number.
3. **Cooling by car class.** Generators stay. Cooling is set per class so those generators land in the window on that class of car.
4. **Front Tire Ducts.** Near-final front band lever. Tuning → Band tuning → Front Tire Ducts. 50% is neutral: soak 1.0 and no extra tire air. 1% warms the tire, soak 1.15, and is only for a front that is still cold of the band. 100% is open: less brake heat into the rim and more tire air, for a front that is still hot. Use either one only after steps 1–3, and only when the miss is larger than that track's floor. It does not cool the rotors, and it does not move rolling, flex, soak, or a locked scale. One front axle. Logged A/B. Rear Tire Ducts is not this lever. Every band log through `20261018-033823-73137-1` was taken at the old default, 1%, so those corner temperatures include the 1.15 soak. A neutral repeat comes before any rolling cut from that discovery. The repeat is `20261018-045617-131243-1`. Soak joules fell about 15%. Settled temperatures rose. The slick corner check follows the regimen in the open card. Belasco sizes corner flex. Hirochi and Automation confirm, and they size an edit only when they run hotter than Belasco. Nordschleife is a lock check. Each track starts with a noise pair. The straight stays the separate 12 km run.

Feeling is the fine adjustment after the numbers sit in the window. Then semi-lock, tester fine-tune, and the final lock.

## You are off track if

- The open card below is undriven and you start the next item.
- Two runs differ in track temp, compound, or car, and you compare them.
- GT3 heat is treated as a verdict.
- A soft-sim PASS is treated as a live verdict.
- Native °C is fed into the Node-Thermal grip bands.
- A locked scale is nudged because a different flag's A/B felt hot.
- The layout-damp A/B is driven on plain Sport. Softness 0.8 remaps to 0.65, under the 0.72 gate, so that damp never applied.
- The log is off, or `layoutDamp` is not 0 on a damp-off run.
- The car was reset mid-stint and `stintKm` is read as the whole run.
- A step is driven whose predicted shift is smaller than that track's noise floor.
- A scale moves on one car. Two of the three voting cars agree first.
- Parked, tire-swap, or post-reset rows are read as run data. `Read-NtfRun.py` drops them.
